WHO IS MATEO SALAZAR?

Mateo Salazar, born September 29th, 1993, is an American Internet vocalist, producer, writer, director and performance artist. Mateo Salazar explores the intersection where pop and alternative meet sonically and has arrived where art and entertainment collide visually. From comedy to action sports, mock reality TV drama to WWF wrestling in his live shows, the world of Mateo Salazar is unlike any artist who has come before him.

Mateo Salazar has released music since 2016, with 63 albums, EPs, singles, or remixes formally on Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music, Amazon Music and now only exclusively on Audiomack. Mateo Salazar's 4th and final album "LUMBERJACK TEARS" comes out on September 24th, 2026 at 12:00am exclusively on Audiomack.

A San Antonio, Texas native, Salazar has emerged as a polymath from many different projects and iterations over the last 10 years. As unpredictable as one artist can be, no one can seem to put their finger on what Mateo Salazar will do next. Unafraid to make you laugh, cry, think profoundly or feel completely uncomfortable for the length of a 4 minute music video, he is on the road to developing his own blueprint for packaging and marketing pop culture in the internet era. Versatile in every sense of the word, Salazar not only explores every type of entertainment but also every type of genre in his music alike. The box he puts himself in is limitless. It has no boundaries. Mateo Salazar has built a multimedia project designed to challenge people's perspective of what art is, and he's not the slightest bit concerned what anyone has to say about it!

— Junior Associate Manager
